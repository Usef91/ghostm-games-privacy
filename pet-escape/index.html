Privacy Policy: Pet Escape: Tap Away Puzzle

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Pet Escape: Tap Away Puzzle ("the game") is made by GHOSTM Games.

We do not collect any personal information.

  • The game does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or any other personal data.
  • The game has no accounts, no chat, and no ads.
  • The game does not use analytics or tracking tools, and it does not share data with third parties.
  • The game works fully offline. Your progress (level, coins, boosters, shelter items and settings) is saved only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.
  • The game has no in-app purchases with real money.

Children. The game is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal information, we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes. If a future version adds features that use data (for example in-app purchases or ads), we will update this policy and the store listing before that version is released.

Contact. For questions, contact us through the developer contact email shown on the game's Amazon Appstore page.
